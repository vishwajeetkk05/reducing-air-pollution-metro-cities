# Theory of Change: Reducing Air Pollution in Metro Cities

## Project Overview
This group project develops a Theory of Change for reducing air pollution in metro cities through coordinated policy interventions, clean technologies, public transport, emission control, monitoring, and public participation.

## Inputs
- Skilled workforce and expertise
- Clean energy resources
- Adequate funding
- Clean technologies and innovation
- Air Quality Monitoring Network (AQI Network & Sensors)

## Activities
- Public awareness campaigns
- Clean and green fuel promotion
- Coordination among agencies and PPPs
- Promotion of public transport
- Subsidization and incentives
- Implementation of emission reduction norms
- Monitoring and evaluation

## Outputs
- Strengthened enforcement and compliance mechanisms
- Urban green cover and dust-control measures established
- Air quality monitoring and evaluation findings generated
- Emission control measures and open-burning enforcement implemented
- Improved public transport infrastructure and services
- EV charging infrastructure and clean mobility support established

## Outcomes
- Increased compliance with environmental laws
- Better governance
- Reduced PM2.5 and PM10 levels
- Improved urban green cover and reduced heat-island effects
- Reduced open-burning violations
- Electrification and modal shift towards sustainable transport

## Impact
Cleaner, healthier, and climate-resilient metro cities.

## Key Assumptions
- Strong political will
- Timely flow of funds
- Effective inter-agency coordination
- Citizen participation
- Stable policy environment
- No major natural or economic disruptions
